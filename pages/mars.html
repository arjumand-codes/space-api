Mars Rover Cameras

Camera Guide

Different rovers support different cameras. If a selected camera returns no images, try All Cameras or use the demo sol.

FHAZ

Front Hazard

Front Hazard Avoidance Camera used for navigation and obstacle detection.

RHAZ

Rear Hazard

Rear Hazard Avoidance Camera used to view terrain behind the rover.

NAVCAM

Navigation

Navigation Camera commonly available on many rover photo dates.

MAST

Mast Camera

Mast-mounted camera used for landscape and science imagery.
