Space Weather Types

What DONKI Tracks

DONKI adds an advanced science-data layer to your project. It is less visual than APOD or EPIC, but it makes the project look more serious, technical, and data-driven.

CME

Coronal Mass

Large expulsions of plasma and magnetic field from the Sun’s corona.

FLR

Solar Flares

Sudden bursts of energy from the Sun that can affect space weather.

GST

Geomagnetic

Geomagnetic storm records related to solar activity and Earth’s magnetosphere.

IPS

Shock Events

Interplanetary shock events detected in space weather monitoring.
